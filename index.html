How to use this

  1. Open a free AI assistant in another tab — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity. Any of them.
  2. Copy a step from your guide and paste it in.
  3. Work through the steps in order. It asks questions; you answer them and hand over your documents.

Expect a few hours across a few days, not minutes. Works on a phone. No account, no payment, and nothing you upload here — the work happens between you and whichever assistant you pick.
